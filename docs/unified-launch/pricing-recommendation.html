Does the arithmetic work?

For a standard US domestic online card, Stripe publishes 2.9% plus $0.30. Pay-as-you-go Stripe Billing adds 0.7% of Billing volume. Model: net = price × (1 − 0.029 − 0.007) − 0.30. Actual cent rounding and extra services can differ; international cards, manual entry surcharges, currency conversion, taxes and disputes are excluded.

How I would validate it

Offer the same $49 monthly price in the initial sales conversations. Diagnose missing value or onboarding friction before discounting. Do not activate hosted features whose per-workspace cost or capacity is still unknown. Recommend a different price if measured delivery costs or customer evidence support it; no automatic annual lock-in or promised grandfathering.
