Compare changes with evidence

Connect a proposed improvement to one primary outcome and its guardrails. Record the baseline period, change or release version, eligible cohort and observation period. Show before and after side by side with the underlying counts. A faster page with more failed actions is not an improvement. A change in a chart alone is an observation, not proof that the release caused it; controlled comparisons must retain assignment and exposure records.

Agents can read the measurements within their current scopes and propose a specific improvement and verification check. Existing authority, approval, cost and release gates still govern the change. Preserve a decision trail of what was tried, what happened and whether to keep or revert it. Do not add a separate general-purpose analytics or experiment platform.

Definitions that prevent misleading results

  • Conversion is a count of distinct eligible entities reaching a defined outcome divided by the explicitly shown eligible cohort. Record the entry event, outcome event, observation window and exclusion rules. Separate time-to-convert from same-period activity counts.
  • Return “no data” for a zero denominator; retain the numerator and denominator. Do not average percentages or mix currencies.
  • Version definitions when eligibility or attribution rules change. Show provider lag, incomplete observations, late events and sampling coverage.
  • Use canonical events for business truth; a browser can report perceived latency but cannot author a won deal or paid invoice by sending telemetry.
  • Use bounded, indexed rollups and rebuild them from source events. Duplicate/reordered callbacks must not inflate the totals.
  • Masking and tenant authorization apply to metrics, drill-down, exports and agent tools. Telemetry contains route patterns and operation references, not message bodies, field contents, email addresses, secrets or full record URLs.
  • Bound telemetry volume and retention; measure its overhead. Provider-specific paid ingestion or storage still follows the existing cost gate.

Acceptance evidence

  • Independent export-based calculations match the dashboard for defined conversion cohorts, zero-data, timezone boundaries, replayed/late events and separated currencies.
  • Introduce a measurable delay and a real failed request in an isolated fixture; the correct metric changes. Remove it and repeat the same measurement. Static screenshots alone do not pass.
  • A change record links exact before/after versions and counts; a guardrail regression is visible even if its primary metric improves.
  • Cross-tenant and masked-field probes fail without revealing counts, aggregates or customer content. Forged browser outcome events cannot change canonical business conversions.
  • Disable telemetry transport: the dashboard reports the gap rather than a false improvement to zero errors/latency. Verify bounded ingestion, rollup rebuild and overhead.
