Detail

1. Where Remold is, in plain words

Remold is live on app.remoldcrm.com. It runs the new build. You can sign in with Google.

But three things are not safe yet. Sign-in still uses a test (staging) setup. No backup is set up that we can find. And the next routine deploy from this Mac would point the site at the old database.

Your live workspace held only the sample data at go-live: the 3 companies and 4 people from the seed file. So you are not yet using Remold as your CRM. Your real contacts are somewhere else.

The approved plan has 29 jobs. Most of them build a big product: social posting, a marketing engine, client invoicing, hosted agents. None of those have a user yet. Both reviewers said the same thing on their own: finish a safe CRM you use every day, then sell a small version of it, and park the rest.

2. Recommendation

Do the milestones below in order. Each one ends with a check that someone other than the builder runs, and that check must be able to fail. Keep all the pack's safety rules: changes that only add, a rollback target, independent checks, explicit gates for spend, sends and charges.

Use one independent check per milestone, with a test that fails before the change and passes after. (Opus noted that slices took 3 to 5 review rounds so far; that pace does not fit small own-use features.)

Before any of this starts, an agent records your scope decision in the decisions log. Without it, the Baserow work sits inside I3 and I9, which formally wait on L0. That is a deadlock in the pack (jobs.json depends; I2 remaining in execution.json).
