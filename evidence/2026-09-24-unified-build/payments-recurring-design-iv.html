Why the distinction is supportable

P4 expressly requires recurring SANDBOX behavior and tests against a proposed readonly policy pending commercial confirmation. C5 anticipates collections under already-authorized recurring commitments. C1 permits narrow financial domain commands. Read together, these support testing one human-accepted, finite obligation whose whole exposure is approved and reserved before activation. Its provider renewals settle that obligation; they do not receive fabricated H0 continuation permits.

C3’s current-authority rule remains intact for every new Remold or agent continuation, bounded or unbounded. The social/Mautic per-send interception clause cannot be used to erase C5’s explicit recurring-payment scope, nor can C5 be used to bypass agent dispatch checks. New activation, expansion, replacement, reactivation or collection-enabling card update is a fresh command under current authority. This interpretation is narrow and must be recorded, not silently generalized.

Conditions retained

  • The new typed commitment belongs to the P4 fixture. An opaque H0 payload hash or billing.collect grant is not recurring-contract enforcement.
  • Fixed account/customer/service version, currency, cadence, finite cycle count, whole monetary exposure, immutable generation and exact receipts must be enforced and tested. Refunds do not replenish exposure; unknown outcomes retain it.
  • Readonly may allow already-accepted settlement and human cancellation in this proposed policy. New activation, expansion, manual retry and card changes refuse.
  • Cancellation stays pending until exact provider state and residual invoice/payment outcomes support completion. A null next-attempt field or local timeout does not prove absence. Replacement remains blocked by unresolved obligations.
  • Independent merchant Dashboard edits are detected, not prevented. Provider disconnect is not cancellation. No claim covers unobserved after-backup effects or unlimited commitments.
