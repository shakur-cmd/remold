Defects

D1 (medium): a job that fails more than 60 minutes after it was due is never alerted, and its stall reports "Resolved". Finished outcomes are timed by scheduledTime (due time), and conditions only alerts when lastAt is within the 60 minute lookback. Repro (watermark.json, "past" phase): a failing job scheduled with a due time 2 hours ago runs and fails. opsAlertFunctions records lastFailed: true, failures: 1, but no alert opens. The realistic case is a scheduler backlog over an hour (the builder saw a 25000-job backlog not drain in 10 minutes) that ends in failures. The stall alert then resolves, and nothing says the job failed. Fix: time outcomes by completion (completedTime on the system row, or the check's own time), not due time.

D2 (low): trailing-dot and a few rare address forms pass the host rule. https://localhost./, https://foo.localhost./, https://metadata.google.internal./ and https://printer.local./ are accepted. So are [::7f00:1], [64:ff9b::…], 198.18.0.1, 224.0.0.1 and 255.255.255.255 (iv-channel-table.txt). The operator sets the URL and notices carry no customer data, so the impact is small. Stripping a trailing dot before the check would close the name cases.

Note (not an I2 defect): telemetry capacity. telemetry:record serializes on one metrics row. Locally it drains at about 60 per second on base and on 117917d alike. Sustained REST traffic above that grows the backlog without limit. After 65 minutes, records would fail "Invalid operational span" and the metrics would be lost. This is pre-existing telemetry design, worth a look before production traffic. Alerts would show it as a stall or background error on telemetry:record, apart from the D1 gap.
