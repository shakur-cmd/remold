Bounded extension question

A supported Symfony custom transport is the final physical-send boundary, including queued Mailer delivery. Mautic’s configured Messenger route hands the serialized message to Symfony’s transport handler. A pre-send rendering event alone is insufficient because authorization could change while a message waits.

The proposed extension has two parts: a higher-priority native campaign listener captures durable event occurrence identity and stamps an immutable Remold intent; a custom transport validates exactly one recipient and consumes the current exact intent permit immediately before its local sink call. Unapproved native send paths fail closed. Provider credentials stay outside Mautic and no alternative network delivery is available. Opus approved this bounded direction conditionally. The bounded final-permit, queue/revocation/process-kill and native open/re-entry proofs have now run; current evidence and remaining scope are in handover.html.
