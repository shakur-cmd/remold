What the hash covers

This is a delivery hash including envelope sender and recipient, not a hash of raw MIME alone. The accepted shape is ASCII wire, CRLF, one recipient, no Cc/Bcc, no attachment, and exactly two multipart/alternative parts: UTF-8 plain text and HTML with quoted-printable or base64 encoding. A separate raw SHA256 identifies the exact accepted attempt. Unsupported shapes fail closed. The excluded metadata is restricted to generated header shapes; no freshness window or full calendar validation is claimed. This deliberately narrow proof is not a general mail parser.

Recovery and limits

The plugin submits raw retry MIME for rechecking by the authority bridge to prevent a modified queue message from borrowing an old receipt. Receipt-only recovery means no repeated MIME POST to the receiver; the bridge may still receive retry bytes for validation. Definite pre-effect busy503 permits only the existing bounded retry. An unknown receiver outcome permits GET receipt lookup only. No receipt means the occurrence remains held, without a second delivery attempt.

Native campaign counters, acknowledgement counts and an opened local pixel do not prove inbox delivery. Automated campaign-version approval mapping, marketing frequency and deduplication policy, click/reply branches, dead-letter and unknown-hold operations, full multi-workspace recovery, resource/load and native comparison, Remold editing, provider delivery and the full S4 gates remain open. Fault switches and raw probes are fixture-only; remove or build-gate them before product use.
