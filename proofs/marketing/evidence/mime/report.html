Exact scope and preserved state

The accepted wire shape is ASCII/CRLF, one recipient, no Cc/Bcc or attachments, and exactly UTF-8 plain-text plus HTML parts using quoted-printable or base64. The delivery hash includes envelope sender/recipient and all stable headers/body bytes. Only the generated Date/Message-ID values and one multipart boundary identity vary; quote style remains covered. A separate raw SHA256 identifies the accepted attempt. No freshness window, complete calendar validation or general-purpose MIME parser is claimed.

The plugin sends retry MIME to the bridge for validation. Receipt-only recovery means no second MIME POST to the receiver; the direct /ack route performs accounting only. The boundary attack is a host-side substitution into actual queued bytes; the unchanged control is worker-produced. The oracle's epilogue and body checks overlap, so they are not counted as two independent proofs.

Old /data/guard.sqlite data and WAL remain unchanged. New mime-guard.sqlite is a separate bounded proof namespace, not a migration or resolution of old holds. Independent reads updated only the old SQLite shared-memory index. The independent run retained 66 of 128 allowed intents without clearing or increasing the bound. The receiver has its own volume, no published port and no owner/H0 credentials.
