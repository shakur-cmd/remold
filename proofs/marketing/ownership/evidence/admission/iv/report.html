Independent check: P2 public form admission

2026-09-26 · scope: proofs/marketing, commit 82cd269 on p2/admission-2026-09-26 · level: SERVICE, isolated local Mautic A/B in the colima-remold-proof Docker context, synthetic data · checker: independent verifier session (Claude Opus 5.5), not the builder · verdict: BOUNDED PASS with two real defects

Your calls

  • Post-publish edits are a real hole, not a theoretical one. I mapped the fields and added a remove-DNC action to the live admitted form after publish. The running edge kept posting, and the owner's name was replaced, the email do-not-contact was removed, and a new contact was created. The forced tracking cookie does not stop this. Choose: re-admit the form before every post (needs a read-only native credential at the edge or a sidecar), or accept a native-side lock (admin role that cannot edit published intake forms). Until one ships, treat any admin edit of an intake form as able to undo this fix.
  • Admission lets a form that saves nothing through. With "save result" off on both fields, the edge still says "Submission received" and Mautic stores NULL for email and first name. Small fix: also refuse saveResult !== true and a publish window that is not open now. I can hand this to the builder as a one-line rule plus a unit test.

What I changed on the tenants (all synthetic)

  • Restored: private/publishing/fixtures.json is byte-identical to before (intake A 34, B 29). The edges were re-set up and serve A 34 and B 29 again. The container IDs and server hash are the same. The configs match except admittedAt. The start times changed. snap-initial.json, snap-restored.json
  • Left behind, as the builder also did: forms A 35 to 67 and B 30 to 47 (the IV variants, still published but not served by any edge), IV contacts with DNC, submissions, and 13 anonymous contacts on A (12 from the live cookie mutants, 1 from an mtc_id control) and 1 on B.
  • Unpublished and inert: campaigns A 145, 146 and B 8; segments A 137 to 139 and B 1; point actions A 1, 2 and B 1. The replay also created unpublished campaigns A 144 and B 7. The point actions had no form filter (my properties format was ignored), so while published they awarded points on any tracked submission: 26 log rows on A, all to IV-created contacts. They never fired for edge posts. Campaign memberships: A +2, B +1. Form A 38 was edited and then reverted (action 9 deleted, mappings cleared, field IDs kept).
  • Incident: db-b was OOM-killed (512 MiB limit) at 11:52:37Z during my cleanup, while the campaign 8 unpublish PATCH returned 500. I restarted the same container. InnoDB crash recovery was clean, and the counts match the snapshot taken 20 s before the crash except +2 audit_log rows. db-b idles at about 448 of 512 MiB, so it is fragile under any load. I cannot prove my request was the only cause. For the two segments on A and one on B, I set is_published=0 by SQL instead of using the API, to avoid loading it again.
  • Not touched: Convex backends on 3450 to 3453 and 3490 to 3491, mail, DNS, TLS, host ports, provider accounts.

What I could not check

  • Cookie and header values as Mautic actually received them on the wire. I proved them by the unit test (fake upstream) and by native effects, not by packet capture.
  • Admission for campaign "decision" routes on other event types (for example email or page decisions naming the form), webhooks beyond the existing form_on_submit callback, and plugin integrations.
  • Many edge replicas behind a load balancer, or native posts that bypass the edge. The edge network is internal-only, but I did not test Mautic's own public form route from outside, because there are no host ports by design.
  • This closes a P2 gap at SERVICE level only. P2 and D0M stay open (importer, core reconciliation, send-time suppression and the others listed in the builder handover).
