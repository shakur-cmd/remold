Finding: concurrent raw posts deadlock inside Mautic

The first three after-admission runs (after, after-r2, after-final) did not lose any identity or suppression. But 1 to 2 of every 4 concurrent posts got a Mautic 500. Accepted counts were A 3, 3, 2 and B 3, 4, 3. The edge answered 502 "Submission could not be confirmed", and no row was written. InnoDB shows the cause (deadlock-innodb-a.txt). Two transactions each hold a shared lock on the forms row, which they took for the foreign-key check on the submission insert. Both then wait to upgrade it for UPDATE forms SET submission_count = submission_count + 1, and MySQL rolls one back. Mapped form 1 did not hit this in the baseline, probably because contact matching spreads the transactions out. I did not retry, because a 500 can follow a commit and a retry could duplicate the submission. Instead the edge sends one native post at a time. After that change both final runs accepted 4 of 4. This only holds per edge process. Several edge replicas, or direct posts to Mautic, could still deadlock.

Still open (not proven here)

  • Admission runs at publish time. If someone maps a field, adds an action or links a campaign to an admitted form after publishing, the running edge would not notice until the next publish. The edge has no native admin credential by design. A periodic re-admission or a native-side lock is still needed.
  • This proof does not cover campaign decisions that reference a form in event properties, rather than as a source. With a null contact they should not fire, but that is not tested.
  • publishing/replay.mjs still posts to /form/1 and expects mapped contact creation. Both are now intentionally refused or 404, so that old replay's submission section is superseded by this one and will fail if rerun.
  • Raw capture moves identity resolution into Remold. The importer on raw forms, core Person reconciliation, send-time suppression in Convex, applyChange attribution, the late-ID cursor, stale consent and the 100-capture cap all remain open from earlier P2 reports. P2 and D0M are not complete.
  • Deployed state now: both public edges run server.mjs c9951a8a…9d85 and serve admitted raw forms A 34 and B 29. intakeFormsId is recorded in private/publishing/fixtures.json.
