Accounting and finality contract

An operation keeps its original maximum reservation in op.reserved and records actual cumulative usage in op.usage. Tenant/global reserved totals track remaining exposure, while spent increases once when an authoritative step receipt gains usage. release() reads the latest operation and returns only remaining exposure and one active slot; released prevents a second decrement. A result arriving after release still records actual spend, holds the org with an anomaly and preserves the late result.

resolveUnknown requires finality: provisional or final. A still-authorized provisional lookup may retry the same logical step key; cancelled/revoked provisional work stays unknown and reserved. Final means a scoped adapter or human operator asserts provider lookup is closed, releases once, and forbids re-sending that step. Each provider proof must establish its real finality/idempotency guarantee; this fake adapter's assertion is not provider certification. There is no invented elapsed-time finality.
