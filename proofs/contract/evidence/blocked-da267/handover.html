Contract boundaries

  • convex/contract.ts contains discriminated record/binding/model/agent scopes, payload and outcome types, delegation subset comparison and the shared deny-first field projector. Direct action still requires a current scoped grant and a payload/version snapshot; human-approved propose mode is separate.
  • Every operation has attributed author/actor/owner, immutable approval, distinct money-minor amount and reservation units, step/maxSteps, lease/fence, exact binding and one-use short permit. Idempotency includes the step; provider fixture keys include the binding namespace. Revocation before permit denies; a previously issued permit may produce one bounded late result.
  • Expired, failed and unknown states have bounded exits. Authoritative absence permits a new currently authorized attempt, never blind resend. Three total attempts, five maximum declared steps, eight per-run fixture units, tenant/global budget and concurrency limits are synthetic bounds, not commercial prices or provider capacity claims.
  • Adapter sessions are scoped to provider/environment/account. Bind derives tenant from a durable provisioning intent. Fixture sessions use independently random tokens, and exports omit those tokens. The dump is internal-only.
  • The fake inbound adapter authenticates HMAC over the actual event/body and retrieves separate authoritative fake state. Backend callback dedup and state effects are transactional, suppression cannot be erased by a later provider subscription/re-import observation, and completed cursor checkpoints never regress.
  • Distinct SIM history, suggestion, report, export, file, provider, REST, MCP, subscription and record shapes contain leakable synthetic values. All apply grant-chain record/object/field masks; filtering, sorting and grouping denied fields refuse. These are not claims that future product surfaces already exist.
