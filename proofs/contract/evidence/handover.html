Failure, settlement and finality contract

Absence applies only to the current unresolved logical step. Historical acceptance receipts remain reconcilable, but absence for a historical settled step cannot finalize or requeue another step. Target validation happens before an idempotent final-absence return. A current-step receipt makes absence invalid; no broad acceptance of historical consumed steps is allowed.

A plain adapter fail report, whether retryable or nonretryable, does not establish that a consumed provider attempt incurred no cost. If any consumed step has no receipt and no authoritative final absence, fail leaves outcomeUnknown and preserves its reservation and active slot. A subsequent receipt or explicit final absence settles once. The cancelled consumed-failure case exercises duplicate fail and duplicate final-absence calls without a dead end or double release.

Provisional absence permits an authorized same-key retry but does not release unknown exposure. Final absence forbids retry and releases once only when no known receipt still lacks usage. A later unexpected acceptance still records truth and cost and raises an anomaly without double-decrementing reserved/active. No elapsed-time rule invents finality. Real provider jobs must prove their finality and idempotency assumptions.

Operations retain their original maximum in reserved and cumulative usage separately. Tenant/global reserved totals represent remaining exposure; spent changes once per accepted step receipt. Release is idempotent and additionally refuses unresolved consumed attempts or any receipt with missing usage. Accepted overruns record actual cost and hold new org spend until operator review.

API and state semantics for adopters

fail({token,id,fence,step,retryable})
  unconsumed rejection → queued, refused or cancelled
  any unresolved consumed attempt → outcomeUnknown; reservation held
unknown({token,id,fence,step})
  unconsumed current permit → no-op; scheduled expiry recovers it
resolveUnknown({token,id,fence,step,absent:true,
                finality:'provisional'|'final'})
  requires current step with no receipt; older same-step consumed fences valid
reconcile({token,id,fence,step,providerRef,usage?,continue?})
  accepted one-step overrun → confirmed + overrun + anomaly hold
  continuing overrun → paused + overrun + anomaly hold
  accepted exhausted continuation → confirmed + continuationRefused
adapterStatus({token,id,fence,step}) → {state,cancel}
permit/consume return current maxUnits and maxRecipients
callback suppression resolves binding.local + purpose + channel
