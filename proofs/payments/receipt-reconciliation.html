Smallest complete identity model

  • One immutable account-scoped receipt identity per refund or credit note. Store provider, environment, account, document, kind, exact provider ID, source reference, optional operation metadata, integer minor amount and status. Duplicate identical receipt is idempotent; reuse across documents or differing amount is refused.
  • Adapter performs bounded, complete authenticated traversal of invoice payments, successful charge refunds and invoice credit notes. Validate account, invoice/charge/payment binding, currency and sum. Partial traversal or mismatch marks reconciliation incomplete; no further monetary permits.
  • Outside pending refunds count as held. A complete traversal refetches each exact refund and credit-note object by ID before status changes. Failed/cancelled refunds count zero only after that refetch. Credit-note void observations retain immutable original amount and record void status. A voided note contributes zero current credit; accepted local receipts remain auditable.
  • Final capacity equals distinct current receipts plus unresolved dispatched operations. A hold stops counting separately only after an exact receipt is bound to that operation. An aggregate alone cannot settle an operation.
  • Callback-first and acknowledgement-first feed the same receipt table. Both produce one adjustment. Incoming truth remains permitted after actor removal/readonly; new authority is checked separately.
  • Authenticated receipt metadata may suggest an operation but is never enough by itself: exact account, document, kind, amount and operation identity must agree. Unknown outcome has no owner-release path.
  • Target generations fence overlapping pulls: starting a new pull closes capacity; an older pull cannot reopen it. The collector is bounded to five pages per list, 30 reads and 500 normalized receipts. Reaching a bound leaves that target incomplete.
  • Fresh pulls provide observed provider state, not an atomic lock over external Dashboard actions. Stripe remains final provider authority; no claim to prevent an outside action between pull and dispatch.
