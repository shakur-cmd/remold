Small implementation boundary

Add a required, explicit continuation branch to the existing sandbox driver, replacing customer creation with these two known bindings. The financial sequence from quote creation onward stays on the accepted adapters. Record continuationOf and a unique continuation evidence filename. A small provider wrapper durably records each write attempt before dispatch and its response receipt before the next step; uncertain attempts remain stopped for review. Persist the new fixture mapping privately for investigation. No generic resumption engine.

Only provably unfinished work runs: the previous financial sequence never passed invoice creation. The old local consumed operation remains preserved and unresolved by this new namespace. New fixture namespaces are not independent tenant isolation: adapter tokens with the same provider/environment/account scope can address both. No production identity or authorization claim is added.

Email and stop boundaries

RFC2606 reserves .invalid for deliberately invalid domain names. Stripe acceptance of that address is unproved until its customer-update readback, and invoice validation can still reject it. If either fails, stop; do not try real addresses or create a throwaway invoice.

Stripe automatic-advancement documentation says auto_advance:false disables invoice emails, retries and reminders, while receipts have separate settings. Keep that flag false at creation/finalization, never invoke /send or receipt_email, and retain credit-note email_type:none. The pinned send endpoint and charge receipt documentation describe no delivery for those test-mode cases; this is not a universal claim about all Stripe email.

On any error, unexpected shape, timeout or process loss, preserve the latch and evidence and stop. Do not repeat accepted writes. A later continuation requires a new reviewed reconciliation. Recurring remains required BLOCKED and the final incomplete exit1 is expected, not a retry signal. Webhook3492 is free; real forwarding has not yet been proved.
