P4 recurring: bounded commitment proposal

2026-09-25 · Design only · No implementation, provider calls or payment-source changes

Next engineering decision

Record the interpretation below, then review a small P4-local human commitment and dedicated subscription fixture. No new owner permission is inferred. Current subscription and invoice/pay refusals stay in place until that design is accepted. Existing live G-pay and shared-contract review gates remain.

Interpretation: every Remold or agent continuation, bounded or unbounded, requires a new current-authority check. A provider renewal within one explicitly accepted, finite human financial commitment settles that commitment; it is not a new Remold job permit. Firing an agent does not cancel an independent human customer contract. This is the proposed P4/C1/C5 interpretation, not a C3 waiver.

Established by source review

Frozen H0 cannot enforce a recurring mandate: its binding grant checks a per-operation amount, one due time and one non-model step; reservations are generic units. Putting a schedule in an opaque payload hash does not validate cadence, lifetime exposure or cancellation. Exact Opus5.5 Max follow-up agrees that a separate bounded human commitment can be tested under the already-authorized sandbox P4 scope. No recurring behavior has been tested here.

Smallest shape

Keep H0 byte-identical. Add one P4-local commitment record plus per-invoice cycle facts. The commitment binds org, exact connected account/customer, immutable accepted service version, fixed price and quantity1, currency, interval, start/end, maximum3 cycles, total money reservation, human approver and epoch, generation, and exact provider schedule/subscription IDs. Use narrow typed intents for activation, payment-method change, cancellation and replacement; a billing.collect or billing.refund grant never implies one of these.

Proposed synthetic fixture: 301 USD minor units per cycle, maximum3 cycles, total903 minor units. These are test values, not a commercial price. Reserve the whole finite exposure before activation. Each cycle is identified by exact account, subscription generation and invoice ID; deduplicate authoritative invoice-payment/PaymentIntent/Charge receipts. Gross paid plus unresolved exposure never exceeds the accepted commitment. Refunds do not replenish it; unknown results do not release it. A failed retry is not a new obligation, and provider attempt_count is not a charge count.

Policy and provider limits

As a labeled proposed-policy sandbox test, existing accepted commitments may continue during readonly while new activation, expansion, reactivation, manual retry and card updates refuse. Incoming truth and authorized human cancellation remain available. Removing the approving human or firing an agent stops new management actions; it does not erase the org’s accepted obligation. Live collection policy remains G-pay.

D/E remain the selected Standard accounts with full Dashboard access. Remold can detect independent merchant changes, not prevent them. Amount/currency/price/phase-count changes, released schedules, a fourth invoice or unknown lineage raise an anomaly and block new Remold actions; remote activity already accepted may continue while cancellation is pending. The cap claim covers Remold-authorized actions, not independent merchant Dashboard actions.

Disconnect does not cancel subscriptions and may remove Remold’s ability to manage them. Preserve cancel-before-disconnect ordering; loss of access is “unobservable, merchant-held,” never “stopped.” Pending invoice items can bill on another subscription, which is why replacement needs an empty complete list. The existing human C1 void path can address eligible old invoices while writable; it is not a generic collection bypass.

Proof sequence after design acceptance

  1. Preserve baseline: freeze a separate additive candidate; keep current54 and original H0 hashes. Demonstrate ordinary billing.collect cannot activate, update or cancel a commitment and existing unsupported routes remain refused.
  2. Local authority/accounting: A/B in one database; tampered accepted version, account, customer, amount, cycles, currency and generation refuse. Concurrent activation cannot double-reserve; unknown activation keeps the entire cap. Hold before activation, remove human authority, release: no dispatch. Hold after permit: record bounded late result, no new permit.
  3. Provider prerequisite: isolated new D/E synthetic customers and fixed price; test clock support on the exact connected accounts must be observed. If unavailable, retain the same proof with a daily interval and actual elapsed time. Never substitute local fake time for provider renewal evidence.
  4. Activation and finite renewal: lose the successful create response, resolve exactly one schedule/subscription, observe first charge and later renewals on A, absent from platform/B. Read exact finite end and show no fourth cycle. For each successful charge, repeat explicit PI/Charge and complete platform fee-list proof; absent invoice fee remains unobserved.
  5. Failed renewal/card/retry: use a documented test method that attaches but declines. Observe actual failed invoice and exact attempts; update the correct subscription default through the verified SetupIntent, then observe a provider retry settling the same invoice without duplicate paid amount. Preserve unknown/in-flight reservations and refuse manual invoice/pay.
  6. Cancellation/replacement races: cancel before and during an attempted retry; show terminal schedule/subscription, open/draft invoice collection state and remaining late/unknown outcomes honestly. Card change after local cancellation request refuses. Replace only after reconciled cutoff; replay signed old-generation notifications and forged B IDs without changing the new commitment.
  7. Verification: record exact request version, provider IDs/account scopes, invoice/PI/Charge receipts, money oracle, signed callback/pull provenance and private journal. Independent reviewer changes amounts and periods and repeats relevant negatives. A failed or inaccessible row stays open.
