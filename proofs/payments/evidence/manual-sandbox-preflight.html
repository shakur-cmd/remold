Stop and recovery rules

The runner is not resumable. Each new process creates a new run ID, H0 operations and idempotency keys, and overwrites sandbox.json. Restarting it may repeat accepted financial effects. Only the deliberate invoice-create response loss and the one refund response-loss test have bounded, same-run recovery. A real timeout, process kill, or lost item/finalize/PaymentIntent/attachment response requires a stop and read-only reconciliation of the exact original account, operation and provider IDs. Retain holds until authoritative receipts resolve them. A reviewed continuation may execute only work proven unfinished; never retry the whole driver automatically.

Evidence is checkpointed after checks, not transactionally after every provider effect. A kill can lose current in-memory evidence and raw webhook captures. The final exit status remains 1 when manual checks pass but recurring is BLOCKED. That status must not trigger a retry. Webhook replay verifies captured signatures within a five-minute freshness window; a long run can encounter an expired early capture. Such a failure does not establish that payment effects were absent.
