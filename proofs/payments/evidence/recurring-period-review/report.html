Use the correct provider fields

Pinned invoice definitions direct service-period consumers to invoice lines. The line object provides service period, parent subscription item, price, quantity and amount. Its complete line list can be read from the invoice snapshot when has_more=false; the full lines endpoint is also documented. Opus had not read the endpoint document fetched during its call; that does not change the smaller complete-embedded-list choice.

Minimal source changes

  1. Bind the one original subscription item during activation readback, checking its exact accepted price/quantity and full item-list completeness. Reconciliation uses the same binding path.
  2. Read one complete non-proration recurring line from the double-read invoice snapshot. Check invoice/account/test mode, subscription/item, price, quantity one, 301 USD minor, no taxes/discounts/credits, and exact invoice totals. Missing, extra or unsupported line facts remain anomalous observations.
  3. Pass explicit normalized line identity and service start/end into the core. Compute index0–2 from accepted cadence in the core, never trust an adapter-supplied index. Keep top-level invoice period separate and allow it to differ. Bind expected first-cycle/subsequent billing reasons as a supported-shape assumption.
  4. Retain all invoice/payment history, including omitted invoice rows, with bounded exact successful-receipt union sums. Separate observed gross from recognized accepted-cycle payments. Different invoice/same cycle, changed line identity, or unsupported periods block clean completion. Late payment time alone does not invalidate a valid service interval.
  5. Keep cancellation/unknown holds, readonly restrictions, replacement generations and closed provider requests. No new resume engine, tax/credit/balance framework or generic subscription route.
