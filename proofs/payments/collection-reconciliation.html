What changed

Independent paid/state callbacks close financial capacity and invalidate older pulls. Bound invoices require a complete reconciliation even before their first callback. Collection counts observed gross payments plus unresolved local holds, subtracts exact credits and pending credit commands, and excludes a hold only after its own exact invoice payment and acknowledgement agree. Void and credit cannot race an unresolved local collection.

Small accounting model

  • The existing adjustment receipt table also holds successful invoice_payment IDs. Account/provider/environment/kind/ID identity is immutable. The exact PI, command digest and amount must agree with the held operation. A known PI cannot appear under another allocation ID or document.
  • The adapter puts SHA256 of the exact operation logical ID in PI metadata before hashing the approved payload. A real loopback HTTP dispatch checks the transmitted digest against the H0-approved payload, then exercises its receipt match.
  • Full bounded pulls read every invoice-payment page and exact object. Successful allocations must equal the PI received amount and charge amount. Partial/shared allocations, pending payments, missing retained paid receipts, changed paid status, or totals inconsistent with the current invoice close reconciliation. No aggregate releases a hold.
  • Capacity is obligation minus gross observed paid, unmatched collections, successful credits and unmatched pending credits. Refunds never reopen collection. Post-payment credits may conservatively under-allow; their real provider semantics remain to be verified.
  • Collection refuses non-open lifecycle states, sent/settled voids, and bound invoices without complete reconciliation. Void and credit refuse unmatched collections. Convex transactions serialize these local checks.
  • A pull’s own observation carries its generation. Competing paid/state/refund/credit callbacks invalidate that generation. New renewal rows start incomplete. No monotonic-max-paid policy is invented.

Limits still open

Stripe invoice-payment terminal status, shared allocation behavior, gross paid after refunds, partial credit-note behavior, canceled PI finality and actual attach/overpayment behavior need SANDBOX replay. Default open PIs requiring a payment method count zero; processing or otherwise unresolved PIs close reconciliation, including an unresolved PI under a canceled allocation. These are conservative proposed rules, not provider proof.

Failed or canceled local collection holds have no automatic release path. A provider-refused void stays pending until a future authoritative settlement path. Outside Dashboard actions after a pull remain non-atomic with local permits; standalone PaymentIntents cannot be assumed to enforce an invoice ceiling. Previously delivered Stripe-hosted URLs can remain payable. This repair does not prove the full quote/subscription/dispute/webhook matrix, enabled second merchant, production credentials, human UI, restore/deploy safety or customer policy.
