Four additive tables hold immutable versioned service terms, human commitments, one-use commands, and exact invoice/payment identities. Synthetic terms are 301 USD minor units per cycle, three cycles, total 903. Adoption reserves full exposure atomically. All Remold/agent continuations, bounded or unbounded, still require current authority.

Activation and card-change consumption require the current human finance role, active session, matching actor epoch, healthy merchant, writable workspace, exact tenant/provider binding and unconsumed command. Service identity and truthful amounts

The original subscription item is bound by a fresh scoped activation read. The invoice must carry one complete non-proration subscription line matching that item, accepted price, quantity one, 301 USD minor and supported totals. The core computes one of three exact service intervals from the accepted plan. Invoice-level accumulation periods may differ and do not authorize a cycle. Core-computed identity is immutable; an incomplete first line stays unidentified until complete, while a complete invalid line stays invalid. Existing cycle claims win; two new same-cycle claimants in one pull receive neither claim.

paidMinor is gross, summed from all retained distinct successful receipt identities, including omitted invoice rows. recognizedMinor only counts receipts that first succeeded against a valid unchanged accepted line, capped301 per unique cycle. The eligibility decision is internal and immutable; pending receipts remain undecided. Reservation is903 minus recognized money while unresolved. A history-regression anomaly does not erase valid earlier recognition:100+201 remains301/602. Changed-line money remains visible without consuming legitimate capacity. Incomplete reads can later establish identity/recognition; existing anomalies still require review and block management. There is no automatic anomaly-clear command.

Per-receipt amount is bounded at100,000,000; at most100 retained invoices per binding and10 receipts per invoice bound the aggregate at100,000,000,000. Unsafe/noninteger amounts refuse. Provider deviations are retained as anomalous rows; tenant/lineage/receipt-ownership integrity failures refuse.

Closed provider interface

  • Activation: exact customer and immutable price, quantity one, three iterations, end_behavior=cancel, automatic collection, disabled automatic tax and proration. Fresh scoped SetupIntent/card and empty balance/pending-item preflight.
  • Card update: only default_payment_method on the bound subscription, resolved through the scoped succeeded off-session SetupIntent.
  • Cancellation: only the bound schedule, with invoice_now=false and prorate=false. Acceptance alone never claims all invoice/payment liabilities stopped.
  • No manual invoice/pay, arbitrary subscription update, generic route dispatch, or new PaymentIntent creation is exposed here.
